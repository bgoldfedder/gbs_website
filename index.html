The practice

Gold Brand Software is the independent consulting practice of Brandon Goldfedder — a software engineer with more than thirty-five years of building systems that have to be right: regulated clinical data, federally funded research platforms, and the long-lived enterprise software a business actually runs on.

Engagements tend to be long and hands-on. I design the system, write production code, deploy it, and stay accountable for it in operation — often for years. That means the person making the architectural calls is the same person who has to live with them, which is the point.

Work spans commercial and government-funded sectors, and has included lead-architect, principal-investigator, and fractional-CTO roles alongside straight development.

How I work

  • Ownership over deliverables. I would rather be accountable for a system in production for a decade than hand over a document and leave. Most of my engagements have outlasted the contract that started them.
  • Enough design, then working software. Sufficient architecture to avoid the mistakes that are expensive to undo, and no more. The rest is learned by building the thing and watching it run.
  • Write down the reasoning, not just the result. Decisions, trade-offs, and the constraints that forced them — recorded in a form the next engineer can actually use. Patterns are a means of communication before they are a means of construction.
  • Small footprint, low coordination cost. One engineer who can carry architecture, implementation, deployment, and operations removes a great deal of hand-off overhead. Where a team is needed, I would rather build and lead a small one.
